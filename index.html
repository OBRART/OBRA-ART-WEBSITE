Duo show
Miriam Dema & Chidy Wayne — Barcelona, 4–7 May 2023

OBRA Art opened its inaugural exhibition in Barcelona in May 2023 with a presentation of works by Miriam Dema and Chidy Wayne.

Miriam Dema currently lives and works in Barcelona. Her work is a tribute to the beauty of imperfection and the generosity of life in moments of splendour. She seeks to represent the presence of humans in everyday elements and nature, depicting scenarios that evoke warmth and relaxation and include elements of Mediterranean culture. She uses wax crayons and oil pastels for their imprecision and roughness, and usually works with oil paint on wooden boards, painting intuitively and from memory.

Chidy Wayne is a Spanish-Guinean multidisciplinary artist based in Barcelona, working across painting, sculpture, illustration and music. Seeking work that is both aesthetic and conceptual, he creates a personal code of signs and motifs using quick, bold strokes that present universal themes of existence, identity and internal conflict — influenced by the artistic avant-garde as well as ancestral and cultural elements.

The exhibition was held in a typical Catalan apartment with a live performance by flamenco guitarist Rafael Ulecia.
